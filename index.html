The story behind Heaven's Sprinkle Cakes

Heaven's Sprinkle Cakes started in a home kitchen with one goal: bake cakes that taste as good as they look, for the people celebrating something real. A first birthday, a graduation, an anniversary, or just a Tuesday that needed cake.

Every order is mixed, baked, and decorated by hand in small batches. No fondant shortcuts, no store-bought mixes, just real butter, real vanilla, and enough sprinkles to make it feel like a party.

Good to know

The practical details before you order.

Home-based bakery with pickup by arrangement. Let us know your area when you enquire and we'll confirm what's possible.

Delivery

Local delivery available on request — let us know your address and event date and we'll quote a delivery fee.

Order requirements

Frequently asked questions

Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll get back to you.

How far in advance should I order?

For a standard cake, 48 hours notice is usually enough. For tiered, fully custom, or wedding-style designs, we recommend at least a week so there's time to plan the details with you.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes — a deposit secures your order date once the design and flavor are confirmed. The balance is due on collection or delivery.

Can I request a custom design or flavor?

Absolutely. Send us a reference photo, theme, or color palette over WhatsApp and we'll let you know what's possible and quote accordingly.

Do you cater for allergies or dietary requirements?

Let us know about any allergies or dietary needs when you enquire. We'll confirm whether we can accommodate the request before you order.

Do you offer delivery, or is it pickup only?

Both. Pickup is by arrangement from our home bakery, and local delivery is available on request for an additional fee based on your location.

Can I change my order after I've paid the deposit?

Minor changes (flavor, size, colors) can usually be accommodated up to a few days before your date. Message us as soon as possible if something changes.
